How the house earns

1 · The payout spread
A trader stakes $10 at 80% payout. Win: they receive $18. Loss: the $10 stays with the platform. At an even win rate the expected margin is 10% of volume — set per asset above.
2 · Real market prices
Entry and expiry prices come from the live Deriv feed, the same feed drawn on the chart. There is no price offset, no candle manipulation, no per-trader rigging — the numbers every trader sees are the market numbers.
3 · Transparent limits
Min/max stake, max open positions and the payout cap apply equally to every account and are visible to traders in the ticket. Exposure is monitored on the dashboard.
